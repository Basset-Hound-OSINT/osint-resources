# Person of Interest Template

This template provides a structured format for documenting OSINT findings about individuals. It covers identity verification, digital footprint, social connections, and activity timelines.

**Reference Sources**: Criminal Intelligence for Analysts, TII Online Investigators Checklist, TII Online Research Framework, Intelligence Analysis Reports

**Important Note**: This template is for legitimate investigative purposes only. Always ensure compliance with applicable privacy laws, terms of service, and ethical guidelines.

---

## Template Checklist

Before finalizing your profile, verify:

- [ ] Identity information is documented with confidence levels
- [ ] All known aliases and usernames are listed
- [ ] Digital footprint is comprehensively mapped
- [ ] Professional background is verified where possible
- [ ] Key connections are identified with relationship types
- [ ] Timeline captures significant activities
- [ ] Patterns of behavior are analyzed
- [ ] Assessment clearly states confidence and gaps
- [ ] Evidence is properly logged and preserved
- [ ] Sensitivity markings are appropriate

## Ethical Considerations

```
ETHICAL GUIDELINES
------------------

This template should be used in accordance with:

1. Legal Compliance
   - Applicable privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
   - Platform terms of service
   - Professional regulations

2. Ethical Standards
   - Legitimate investigative purpose
   - Proportionality of investigation to concern
   - Minimization of collateral information collection

3. Data Handling
   - Secure storage of collected information
   - Limited retention periods
   - Access controls

4. Professional Standards
   - Accuracy verification before inclusion
   - Clear distinction between fact and inference
   - Regular review and updating
```
