# 🕵️ Introduction

Welcome to **The OSINT Resource Index** — a structured collection of open-source intelligence tools, techniques, and databases organized by topic and investigative focus.

This resource is designed for analysts, journalists, investigators, researchers, and cybersecurity professionals seeking to efficiently locate, analyze, and verify publicly available information.
Its purpose is not to promote invasive activity, but to **centralize legitimate, ethical, and educational OSINT resources** used in research, digital verification, and threat intelligence.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

**Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)** involves gathering and analyzing publicly accessible data from across the web — from government records and social platforms to leaked databases and network analysis tools.

Because these tools are dispersed across many domains and communities, this index simplifies discovery by organizing them by **data type**, **methodology**, and **domain of application**.

---

## 📚 Major Categories

This site is divided into the following key areas of OSINT practice:

* **Data Types & Identifiers** — Tracing and verifying people, contact info, vehicles, or travel data.
* **Discovery Tools** — Search engines, mapping tools, and custom query utilities.
* **Social Presence / Networks** — Investigating accounts and activity across major social platforms.
* **File & Media Analysis** — Examining, extracting, or authenticating data from documents, images, videos, or code.
* **Public Records** — Accessing and analyzing business, property, and government databases.
* **Political, Economic, and Financial Intelligence** — Exploring politics, market activity, and digital currencies.
* **Breach & Leak Intelligence** — Researching compromised data, credentials, and cryptographic systems.
* **Specialized Environments** — Investigating within mobile, dark web, IoT, and radio ecosystems.
* **Artificial Intelligence** — Leveraging AI-driven tools for OSINT discovery and analysis.
* **Offensive Security** — Reconnaissance and infrastructure-focused intelligence gathering.
* **Sock Puppets** — Managing investigative identities, virtual environments, and operational security.
* **Intelligence Management, Methods, & Reporting** — Structuring workflows, automating research, and producing analytical reports.
